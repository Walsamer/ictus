# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in Ictus to be a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or
expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive. Critique ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith; ask questions before assuming intent.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Keep technical disagreement focused on the architecture and the evidence.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Discriminatory comments or jokes.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues or pull requests.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions)
and when an individual is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through GitHub.
Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions and comments that violate
this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour
they deem inappropriate.
